# 金狗 Design Language — LOCKED 8/6

One rule above all: every visual reads as an artifact from the same shrine. If a card could belong to another token, it fails.

## Core tokens
- **Colors:** Imperial Red `#8B0000` (deep) / Bright Red `#C41E1E` (accents), Gold `#D4AF37` (metallic gradient to `#F5D76E`), Paper Cream `#F5EFE0`, Ink Black `#0A0A0A`. Nothing else. No blues, no greens, no white-white.
- **The dog:** the glossy medallion render (brand/ canonical assets) is the ONLY dog. Never the old plaque art, never AI-restyled variants.
- **Type:** serif/display for English headlines (site's existing face), heavy CJK for Chinese (Noto Serif SC bold). Chinese is never smaller than English — equal or larger. Chinese is content, not decoration.
- **Stamps & seals:** red square seal stamps (链上为证, 金狗已到, 真金到手) bottom-left or bottom-right corner. Every card carries exactly one seal.
- **Couplets:** 打狗人空手而归 / 持狗者坐等分金 flank vertically when the layout allows. Vertical text = shrine energy.
- **Numbers:** gold metallic, huge, always the hero. Weight (oz/grams) before dollars.
- **Texture:** paper grain on cream surfaces, subtle vignette on red. Flat = sticker = wrong.

## Card grammar (every card is one of these 5)
1. **Ledger** (cream paper, ingot wall, all-time oz) — daily 8:08
2. **Receipt** (dark, tx-hash strip, single payout proof) — weekly
3. **Proclamation** (red paper, gold text, announcement/milestone)
4. **Lore** (black bg, medallion centered, one couplet, cinematic)
5. **Macro** (news riff: gold price, binance, RWA — quote-post visual)

## Layout constants
- 1600x900 for timeline cards, 1080x1080 for square/reply
- Medallion watermark 64px in a corner on every card
- jingou.gold bottom center, small, always
- One idea per card. Two numbers max per card.

## Voice pairing
- Caption always bilingual, dog speaks first person, never "we"
- Locked tags: $金狗 + $GOLDDOG every post; #金狗 #BNBChain; $XAUt/$BNB only on macro posts

## SLOGAN — LOCKED 8/6 (Silk)
**真金不怕火炼 · Real gold fears no fire** is THE slogan and the X bio lead. It outranks every other line. Couplets (打狗人空手而归 / 持狗者坐等分金) are lore support, not the headline. Every card, video outro, og description, and bio leads with or features the slogan. Site titles + meta updated 8/6.
